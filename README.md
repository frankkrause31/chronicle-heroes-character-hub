## What Chronicle of Heroes Does

Chronicle of Heroes gives players and game masters a dedicated web-based place to manage D&D character records. Key details such as hero statistics, carried gear, and personal lore can be kept together instead of being scattered across separate notes.

Because tabletop characters evolve from session to session, the application provides a straightforward way to revise campaign information as it changes. Groups can use the organized records when preparing adventures, checking character details, or playing at the table.

---

## Core Capabilities

- Keep track of essential character statistics
- Manage inventories and currently carried equipment
- Store backstory, lore, and other notes about a hero
- Arrange character information for tabletop RPG campaigns
- Maintain records for multiple heroes and their campaign details
- Access the application through a web browser
- Refer to character records during preparation and live play

## Using the Application

To begin managing a campaign record:

1. Launch Chronicle of Heroes in a browser.
2. Choose an existing hero or create a new record.
3. Fill in the character's statistics and relevant campaign details.
4. Add or revise inventory entries whenever equipment changes.
5. Document backstory, lore, and significant character developments.
6. Refresh the record during sessions or after they conclude.

The resulting information can serve as a reference for encounter preparation, campaign-history reviews, and ongoing party management.
